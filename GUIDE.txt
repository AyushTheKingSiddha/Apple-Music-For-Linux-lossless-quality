APPLE MUSIC (LOSSLESS) ON UBUNTU
=================================

You need:
- Ubuntu with an Apple Music subscription
- Internet, about 3 GB free space, and 20 minutes
- This folder, containing setup.sh and GUIDE.txt
- The Apple Music 4.7.0 .apk file (see next step)


GET APPLE MUSIC 4.7.0
---------------------
Newer versions refuse to sign in on Linux, so you need exactly 4.7.0.
1. Open  https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/apple/apple-music/apple-music-4-7-0-release/
2. Pick the stable 4.7.0 (not "beta") with "x86_64" in the variant (nodpi).
3. Ignore the big ad buttons; use the real "Download APK" button.
4. Move the downloaded .apk file into this folder, next to setup.sh.

Note: lossless works up to 24-bit/48 kHz. Hi-Res (96/192 kHz) is not possible on Linux.


BEFORE YOU START: set a password on your Apple account
-------------------------------------------------------
This Apple Music version asks for an email AND a password.
If you always signed in with just an email code, you have no password yet:
1. In your browser, open  https://account.apple.com
2. Click "Forgot password?" and enter your email.
3. Follow the steps to set a password. Remember it.


RUN THE SCRIPT
--------------
1. Put this folder in your Home folder (the folder named after you).
2. Right-click inside the folder and choose "Open in Terminal".
3. Type this and press Enter:

       bash setup.sh

4. Type your laptop password when asked. Nothing shows on screen while
   you type a password; that is normal. Press Enter.
5. Wait. Green lines show progress. When it says "All done!", it worked.
   If you see red "ERROR", read the message: it says what to do.
   If you're still stuck, send a photo of the screen to whoever gave you this.


AFTER THE SCRIPT
----------------
1. Open "Apple Music" from your app menu.
2. Sign in with your Apple account email and the password from above.
   If it asks for a 6-digit code, enter the code sent to your phone or email.
3. In Apple Music, tap the three dots (top right) > Settings > Audio Quality:
   turn on "Lossless" for streaming and downloads.

IMPORTANT: never update Apple Music. Newer versions refuse to sign in on Linux.
If the Play Store ever opens, search Apple Music, tap the three dots (top right),
and untick "Enable auto update".


EVERY DAY
---------
Just open "Apple Music" from the app menu. Nothing else is needed.
The first launch after a restart can take a minute.


IF SOMETHING BREAKS
-------------------
- Black screen or won't open: restart the laptop and try again.
- "You're offline" in Apple Music: open Terminal and run
      sudo iptables -P FORWARD ACCEPT
  then close and reopen Apple Music.
- To remove everything:
      waydroid session stop; sudo apt remove waydroid; sudo rm -rf /var/lib/waydroid ~/.local/share/waydroid
